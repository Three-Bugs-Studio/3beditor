# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome newcomers, give helpful feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not allowed, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@DongDuong2001](https://github.com/DongDuong2001), through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Three-Bugs-Studio/3beditor/security/advisories/new) or by asking on an issue for a private contact. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may ban a contributor for repeated or serious violations.
