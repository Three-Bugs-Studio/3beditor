# Sound effects

All five are CC0 1.0 (public domain) from Freesound: free for commercial use, no credit needed. They were picked for gym reels from the CC0 pack in [ghost-editor](https://github.com/kurbaitaev/ghost-editor) (MIT) and converted to 48 kHz stereo.

| name | use it for | source |
|---|---|---|
| `boom` | the PR rep or final reveal, once per video | "Deep Boom" by Za-Games, freesound.org/s/539968 |
| `clang` | plates or a bar hitting the floor | "Metallic Clang" by jasonLON, freesound.org/s/125404 |
| `whoosh` | under a whip transition, starting just before the cut | "Backwards Whoosh" by SlavicMagic, freesound.org/s/446010 |
| `riser` | the 2 s build-up before the heaviest set | "Riser sound effect short" by syntheffects, freesound.org/s/685256 |
| `glitch` | under a glitch transition | "Glitch SFX - 1 sek" by h0p1r4e7o, freesound.org/s/673773 |
