# Privacy policy

3beditor runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, store or send any personal data, and Three Bugs Studio runs no server for it.

- **Your videos, photos and music** are read from the folder you choose and processed locally with FFmpeg. Working files go to your computer's temporary folder; only the finished video is saved next to your files. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Reference links** (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) are opened in Claude's browser so Claude can watch the style. Nothing is downloaded, and none of your files are sent to those sites.
- **Optional speech captions** (faster-whisper) run locally. On first use the model is downloaded from Hugging Face; your audio is not sent anywhere.
- What you type to Claude is handled by Anthropic under its own privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Three-Bugs-Studio/3beditor/issues
